# Opening
Answer within the greeting script: brand, your first name, "How can I help you today?" On chat, send the first response within the queue threshold (30 seconds Chat Care, 45 seconds Orders) even if the full answer takes longer.

# Hold
Ask permission before placing a customer on hold and say how long it will take. Return every 60 seconds. If a hold will exceed two minutes, offer a callback instead. Long hold is a leading cause of low CSAT and of abandons on transfer.

# Empathy statements
Acknowledge the problem before solving it: "I can see why that is frustrating; let me sort this out for you." Avoid scripted apologies repeated more than once.

# Known issues
When a known issue is published (for example an app release defect), use the approved known-issue macro: explain the issue, the workaround and when a fix is expected. Do not promise dates that are not in the macro.

# Closing
Summarise what was done and what happens next, confirm the customer has nothing else, and thank them. On chat, wait 60 seconds for a reply before closing an idle session.

# Transfers
Transfer only when the receiving team can resolve the issue. Warm-transfer (introduce the customer) on voice. High transfer rates point to a skills or routing gap and are reviewed monthly.
