# Northwind Telecom (Gold)
- Billing (voice): 80% answered within 20 seconds, measured monthly; abandon rate below 5%; AHT target 360 seconds.
- Technical Support (voice): 80/20; AHT target 540 seconds.
- Chat Care (chat): 80% first response within 30 seconds.
- Penalty: 2% of the monthly invoice for each queue below target; earn-back if the next two months exceed target.

# Halcyon Insurance (Silver)
- Claims (voice): 80% answered within 30 seconds; AHT target 600 seconds.
- Policy Servicing (email): 90% first response within 4 business hours.
- Penalty: 1.5% per queue below target.

# Kestrel Retail (Gold)
- Orders (chat): 80% first response within 45 seconds.
- Returns (email): 90% first response within 4 business hours.
- Sale events: targets apply during sales; the client must share sale dates at least three weeks ahead (PLAYBOOK-KES-01).

# Meridian Bank (Platinum)
- Cards (voice): 85% answered within 20 seconds; abandon below 4%; AHT target 300 seconds.
- KYC Back Office: 95% completed within 10 business hours.
- Penalty: 3% per queue below target; two consecutive misses trigger a service improvement plan.

# Measurement rules
Service level excludes short abandons of 5 seconds or less. Email and back-office service levels are measured in business hours. Reports are reconciled against the source systems before sharing with clients.
