# Sampling
Quality analysts audit about 3% of handled voice, chat and email contacts each month, at least eight contacts per agent. New joiners in their first 90 days are audited at double the rate.

# Parameters and weights
| Parameter | What is checked | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting | Branded opening, own name, offer to help | 10% |
| Verification | Identity verified before account details are discussed | 20% |
| Resolution | Correct fix, correct disposition, no false promises | 30% |
| Empathy | Acknowledges the problem, tone, active listening | 25% |
| Compliance | Mandatory disclosures, data protection, call closure | 15% |
The total score is out of 100. Target: 85 or above.

# Fatal errors
A fatal error sets the audit score to a maximum of 50 regardless of other parameters: sharing account information without verification, rude or abusive language, giving incorrect financial or policy information that could cause loss, or deliberately disconnecting a customer. Any fatal error is reported to the team leader the same day.

# Coaching
Agents below 80 for two consecutive weeks get a coaching plan. Coaching sessions are scheduled in intervals with surplus staffing (SOP-WFM-01) and must not be scheduled in the morning peak.

# Calibration
Quality analysts, team leaders and the client's quality contact calibrate weekly on five shared contacts. Variance between scorers should stay within 5 points.

# Link to outcomes
Agents with higher quality scores have higher CSAT and fewer repeat contacts. Low resolution scores are the strongest predictor of repeat contacts within seven days.
