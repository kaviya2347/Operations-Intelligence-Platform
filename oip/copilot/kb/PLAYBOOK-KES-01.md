# Why
Kestrel's sales drive a sharp rise in order chats during the sale and a second wave of returns emails about a week later. Without preparation, chat service level collapses on the busiest day and email backlog builds for a week.

# Three weeks before
- Get sale dates, expected traffic and promotion mechanics from Kestrel.
- Add a promotion uplift to the forecast. History: order chats rose about 60% during the May sale; the forecast uplift of 35% was too low. Use at least 50% for a four-day sale.
- Plan returns uplift for days 7 to 13 after the sale starts (about 40%).

# One week before
- Roster additional heads for Orders chat; cross-skill Returns email agents onto chat for the sale days.
- Approve overtime in advance; freeze leave for sale days.
- Publish sale FAQs and macros (delivery timelines, payment failures, cancellations).

# During the sale
- RTA monitors Orders every 15 minutes; use overflow from Returns email when chat breaches (SOP-OPS-03).
- Raise chat concurrency to three for simple order-status chats only, with team leader approval.

# After the sale
- Move agents back to Returns email for the returns wave.
- Review forecast error and update the uplift for the next event.
