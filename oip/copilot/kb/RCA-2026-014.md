# Summary
From early April 2026, Northwind Billing missed its 80/20 service level repeatedly in the same half-hour intervals. A Pareto analysis showed that the four intervals from 10:00 to 12:00 produced the largest share of statistically significant breaches, with weekdays affected far more than weekends. The breaches began when a new roster cycle took effect on 6 April.

# Data reviewed
Breach records for the quarter by interval, weekday and shift; WFM forecast, required and scheduled agents; staffed agents from real-time adherence; handle time against plan.

# Candidate causes tested
- Staffing shortfall: staffed agents against the Erlang C requirement.
- Volume spike: actual volume against forecast.
- Handle-time inflation: actual against planning AHT.
In the hotspot intervals, staffing was the primary cause of most breaches. Volume was close to forecast on most days and handle time was normal. Staffed agents in the hotspot intervals averaged about 7 against about 9 required.

# Five whys
1. Why did 10:00 to 12:00 breach? Too few agents were logged in for the volume.
2. Why too few? Scheduled heads in those intervals fell well below requirement.
3. Why? The new roster moved the morning shift start to 10:30.
4. Why was that not caught? The roster was not compared with interval requirements before publishing.
5. Why? Roster change control (SOP-WFM-02) was not applied to the cycle change.

# Recommendations
1. Move four rostered heads to a 09:30 start on weekdays, or add a 09:30 to 13:30 part-time shift.
2. Re-apply roster change control: no interval below 95% of required heads without approval.
3. Keep coaching, meetings and training out of 10:00 to 12:00 (SOP-WFM-01).
4. Activate callback for Billing when estimated wait passes 90 seconds during the morning peak.
5. Watch 18:00 to 19:30, where coverage also fell after the roster change.

# Expected effect
An Erlang C estimate with actual volume and handle time puts the hotspot intervals back above target once staffed to requirement, lifting the queue's monthly service level by about two to three points.
