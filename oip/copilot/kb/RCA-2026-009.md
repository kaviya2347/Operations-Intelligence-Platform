# Summary
Chat Care CSAT (top-2 box) fell from about 80% to between 42% and 51% from 23 to 29 March 2026. Volume and service level were normal. The cause was a defect in Northwind's mobile app release of 22 March that broke the bill-download flow; customers contacting chat could not be helped because agents had no workaround.

# Data reviewed
Daily CSAT by queue, survey verbatims, reason codes, handle time and the client's release calendar.

# Findings
- The drop was confined to Chat Care; Billing voice CSAT was unaffected.
- Contacts with reason "Bill download" rose and their CSAT fell the most.
- Handle time rose slightly as agents tried manual fixes.
- Service level and staffing were on target, so the problem was not capacity.

# Five whys
1. Why did CSAT drop? Customers could not download bills and left unsatisfied.
2. Why could agents not help? There was no workaround or known-issue guidance.
3. Why was there no guidance? The client's release notes did not reach the delivery centre.
4. Why not? No release-communication step existed between the client and the centre.
5. Why? The account governance checklist did not include product releases.

# Actions
1. Known-issue macro published on 26 March with the web-portal workaround (done).
2. Client added the centre to its release-notes distribution list (done).
3. CSAT anomaly alert per queue added to the operations intelligence platform, so drops are flagged the next day instead of in the weekly review (done).
4. Release readiness added to the monthly account governance checklist.

# Result
CSAT returned to normal on 30 March after the client's hotfix.
